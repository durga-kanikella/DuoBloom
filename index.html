Daily targets

XP and pass threshold

The pass threshold is calculated as maximum possible daily XP − deduction. Example: max 100 XP and deduction 20 means 80 XP passes; below 80 is a lost day.

Rules, milestones & body goals

Nothing here is fixed: day counts decide your punishment and milestone periods. Changes save automatically.

Milestones

Add a milestone for its starting month. It stays active across future months until achieved; its status changes colour to show how far behind schedule it is. The owner can then record a reward from the other person's piggy bank.

Optional habits · no XP

Each profile can have its own habits. Add more anytime.

Backup & restore

Keep a full backup of your settings, check-ins, and pet collections, or export check-in logs to a spreadsheet you can open and edit in Excel.

Full backup restores settings, check-ins, and pet items. CSV import merges check-ins by profile and date; matching dates are overwritten, other dates are kept. Excel CSV files are supported; ordinary .xlsx workbooks are not imported directly.

Shared across devices

This prototype saves to this browser only. Sharing one website link does not provide live shared storage: for both people to update simultaneously across devices, a shared Supabase database and safe access rules must be connected. Do not put a secret/service-role key in browser code.
